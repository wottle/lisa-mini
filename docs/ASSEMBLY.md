# Assembly Instructions

There are two designs. Pick one and follow its guide:

- [Design A: ESFloppy on back](design_a/ASSEMBLY.md) -- leaves the
  LisaFPGA untouched
- [Design B: ESFloppy on front](design_b/ASSEMBLY.md) -- moves the ESFloppy
  screen, buttons and a lit power button to the front; requires soldering
  to the LisaFPGA

See the [README](../README.md#choose-your-design) for a comparison.
